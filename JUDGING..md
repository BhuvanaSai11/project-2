# Judging and Scoring Methodology

## Overview
The platform implements a structured workflow for evaluating submissions once the submission window closes. This process is designed for the Sankalp hackathon evaluation experience, including Track A - Students Track.

## Scoring Strategy (T1-Core)
* **Role Separation:** Judges are assigned to specific tracks or projects without administrative privileges.
* **Scoring Criteria:** Judges evaluate submissions on a standardized numerical scale based on functionality, completeness, and adherence to requirements.
* **Integrity:** Prevents unauthorized score modifications and ensures isolated evaluation metrics per judge.
* **Track Allocation:** Project evaluation can be segmented by track, such as Track A - Students Track.

## Evaluation Flow
1. Submission deadline closes.
2. Judges review all entries within the assigned track.
3. Scores are recorded and normalized.
4. Results are published to participants and organizers.
